# Week 03: Consistency, Not Talent (and Frame, Not Upstage)

**Track:** General AI Fluency  
**Phase:** Foundations  
**Duration:** 90 min  
**Focus:** AI Judgment, Visual Identity & Portfolio Image Curation  

---

## Executive Summary

The central theme of Week 03 is **AI Judgment**: telling good output from bad, exercising restraint, and making simple, intentional decisions that elevate a software engineer's portfolio from amateur to professional without needing graphic design talent.

When working with generative AI, it is trivial to generate hundreds of shiny images or complex UI designs in minutes. The real engineering skill is **curation and judgment**:
1. Making a few consistent decisions (typography, colors, logo) and sticking to them.
2. Enforcing the golden rule of portfolios: **the design frames the work, it never upstages it**.
3. Knowing when authentic screenshots of real code and live applications outperform any AI-generated visual asset.

## Key Pillars of Week 03

### 1. Simple Choices that Feel Intentional (Consistency over Talent)
- **Typography:** `Space Grotesk` (Headings) + `Inter` (Body).
- **Color Palette:** 4 purpose-driven colors (#4F46E5 Primary, #111827 Text, #F9FAFB Surface, #10B981 Accent).
- **Logo & Favicon:** Clean geometric brand mark.
- **Rule of Thumb:** Make decisions once, record them in project instructions, and never deviate.

### 2. Frame, Not Upstage
- The portfolio background and UI chrome stay quiet, minimal, and modern.
- All visual energy is directed toward the project evidence (architecture diagrams, dashboards, API documentation, live UI).

### 3. Kill Your Darlings: AI Image Curation
- **Rejection Policy:** Reject flashy AI-generated illustrations (e.g., futuristic room with floating holograms). They lack evidence value and harm credibility.
- **Keeper Policy:** Keep authentic screenshots of deployed systems (Home Champion, Darsy LMS, Hospital API, Tomory, etc.).
- **AI Asset Boundary:** AI visual generation is restricted strictly to decorative background meshes or subtle gradients using a locked style prompt.

### 4. Through-Line & Messaging
- **One-Line Claim:** *"I build thoughtful full-stack web applications that combine strong engineering with exceptional user experience."*
- **Primary Call to Action (CTA):** *"Connect with me on LinkedIn."*
